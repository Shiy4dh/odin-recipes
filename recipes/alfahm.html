Alfahm Recipe

Here is a quick and simple recipe for Alfahm (Arabian Grilled Chicken).

ALfahm is an arabian dish that consist of grilled chicken

It is seasoned with pepper and minimal masala.

Ingredients

  • Chicken : 1KG
  • Marinade Base
  • Spice Mix

Steps:

  1. Make the Paste: Blend the onion, tomatoes, garlic, ginger, green chilies, lemon juice, yogurt, and olive oil into a smooth paste.
  2. 1. Mix Spices: Mix the blended paste with all the dry spice powders and salt.
  3. 1. Marinate: Coat the chicken thoroughly with the marinade, forcing it into the gashes. Marinate in the fridge for at least 3 hours (preferably overnight).
    4. Grill
    1. Charcoal (Authentic): Grill over medium coals for 25–30 minutes, flipping occasionally, until charred and cooked through.
    2. Oven: Bake in a preheated oven at 200°C (400°F) for 35–40 minutes, flipping halfway.
  5. Baste: Brush with melted butter or oil during the last 5 minutes of grilling for extra juiciness.
