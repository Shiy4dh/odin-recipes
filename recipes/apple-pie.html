Apple Pie Recipe

Here is a quick and simple recipe for Apple Pie

This apple pie baked Brie combines gooey cheese with sweet apples, cinnamon, and pecans for a crowd-pleasing appetizer that’s ready in just 20 minutes.

Serve it with crackers and fresh apple slices.

Ingredients

  • 1 (8-ounce) wheel brie cheese, scored
  • 1 cup apple pie filling
  • 1/4 cup chopped pecans
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon

Steps:

  1. Preheat the oven to 375 degrees F (190 degrees C).
  2. Place Brie in a small baking dish, cover with apple pie filling, and top with pecans and cinnamon.
  3. 1Bake in the preheated oven until Brie is melted through, 10 to 15 minutes.
  4. Serve immediately with crackers and sliced apples.
