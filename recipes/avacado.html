Avacado Smoothie Recipe

Here is a quick and simple recipe for Apple Pie

TThis avocado smoothie is thick, creamy, and delicious. Makes a great breakfast smoothie.

Ingredients

  • 1 ripe avocado, halved and pitted
  • 1 cup milk
  • ½ cup vanilla yogurt
  • 3 tablespoons honey and 8 ice cubes

Steps:

  1. Scoop avocado flesh into a blender; add milk, yogurt, honey, and ice cubes. Blend ingredients until smooth.
